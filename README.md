Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence, at its core, is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are designed to think like humans and mimic their actions. The realms of AI encompass various subfields, including machine learning, natural language processing, and robotics. These technologies allow computers to perform tasks that typically require human intellect, such as understanding natural language, recognizing patterns, and making decisions.
